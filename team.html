Our story

TechPot started from a simple observation: most money mistakes happen not because people are careless, but because the rules are hard to find and harder to understand.

So we explain them in the language people actually speak, starting from the official source, with a qualified accountant reading the numbers. Finance comes first. Technology and AI come second, as tools that make the explanations clearer and keep you safe online.
