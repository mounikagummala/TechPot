Who can do what

Some financial activities are regulated in India.

Regulated activities

Investment advice needs SEBI registration. Selling insurance needs an IRDAI licence. Tax return filing and certain professional work need the right qualification and, where required, registration.

TechPot’s explainers, tools and videos are educational. Unless a registration is listed here, nothing on this website is regulated advice.

    Common questions

    Can TechPot tell me which fund or policy to buy?

    No. We explain how products work and what to compare. Choosing a specific product for you is personal advice, which needs a registered adviser or licensed insurance professional.

    Is the content in Telugu or English?

    Both. Videos are in conversational Telugu with common English words. This website is mostly in English so official terms stay clear.

    Who checks the facts?

    Every explainer starts from an official source and is reviewed by a qualified Cost and Management Accountant before it is published.

    Do you charge for anything?

    The videos, guides and calculators are free. If paid services are ever added, they will be described here with the credentials behind them.

    What if I find a mistake?

    Tell us on Instagram or through the contact page. Rules change, and we would rather correct a page quickly than leave it wrong.
