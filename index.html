About TechPot

TechPot is a finance-first education brand for families across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, and for anyone in India who wants money rules explained simply.

We turn official rules from the RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and the Income Tax Department into short videos, guides and calculators. Technology and AI come second: they help us explain faster and help you stay safe online.
